ContextFlow Demo Conference Reimbursement Policy

1. Conference registration is reimbursable up to INR 25,000.
2. The claimed amount must not exceed the amount actually paid.
3. Payment proof is mandatory for every reimbursed expense.
4. Hotel reimbursement requires a valid hotel invoice.
5. Hotel reimbursement also requires travel proof such as a boarding pass.
6. Evidence must be dated within 60 days of submission.
7. Employee identity must match the claim owner.
8. Bank details may be used only for reimbursement payment to the approved ExpenseHub portal.
9. Protected bank details must not be disclosed to third-party verification destinations.
10. Attachments must be PDF or supported image evidence.
11. A claim is complete only when the portal returns a receipt and the submitted amount is independently verified.
